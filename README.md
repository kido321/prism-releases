# Prism for Mac

Prism is a projection mapping studio for shaping visuals around real surfaces.

**[Download Prism 1.7.0 for Mac](https://github.com/kido321/prism-releases/releases/download/v1.7.0/Prism-1.7.0-mac-arm64.dmg)**

Requires an **Apple Silicon Mac** and **macOS 12 or later**. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

[Release notes, ZIP download, and checksums](https://github.com/kido321/prism-releases/releases/tag/v1.7.0) · [Visit the Prism website](https://www.prismap.app)

## Install

1. Download and open the DMG.
2. Drag **Prism** into **Applications**.
3. Open Prism from Applications and sign in with the code sent to your email.

## Trial and subscription

Start a **14-day trial of the full app**, with **no card required**. The trial begins when you choose to start it in Prism.

- **$19 USD per month** or **$190 USD per year**, plus applicable taxes.
- Full app access on up to **two computers** per account.
- Up to **seven days offline** after an online license refresh, limited by your remaining trial or paid access.

Initial sign-in and activation require an internet connection. Your projects and media stay on your computer.
